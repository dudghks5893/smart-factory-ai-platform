# PROJECT DEMO SOP — NOT AN ACTUAL FACTORY PROCEDURE

This fictional procedure demonstrates anomaly handling and escalation. It is not an approved
factory instruction.

## Immediate containment

When the AI result is anomaly, stop the affected item from moving to the accepted-product area.
Place it in the clearly marked demonstration quarantine tray and retain the inspection identifier.
Do not discard, rework, or classify the item based only on the AI result.

## Reinspection

Confirm that the camera and lighting checks are satisfactory. Rotate the item to the documented
inspection orientation and capture one new image. Reinspection creates a separate inspection record;
do not overwrite the original result.

If the second result is normal, keep both inspection records and request human review before release.
If the second result is anomaly, keep the item quarantined and escalate it to the designated demo
quality reviewer.

## Escalation record

Provide the reviewer with both inspection identifiers, timestamps, anomaly scores, thresholds, model
lineage, and the equipment-check outcome. The reviewer records the final disposition outside this
demo AI system because the current database contains predictions, not ground-truth defect decisions.
