# PROJECT DEMO SOP — NOT AN ACTUAL FACTORY PROCEDURE

This document is fictional demonstration content for the SmartFactory AI Quality Platform. It must
not be used as an approved factory procedure.

## Pre-inspection camera check

Before visual inspection begins, verify that the camera lens is clean, the mounting bracket is
secure, and the configured illumination is on. Capture one reference image and confirm that the
metal nut is centered and in focus.

If the reference image is blurred, obstructed, or incorrectly illuminated, pause automated
inspection. Clean or secure the equipment, capture a new reference image, and resume only after the
image is clear.

## Routine visual inspection

Present one metal nut at a time in the configured inspection position. Submit the image to the AI
inspection service and record the returned inspection identifier, anomaly score, threshold, and
normal or anomaly prediction.

An AI anomaly prediction is a screening result, not a confirmed defect classification. The system
does not identify a defect type and a human reviewer must follow the anomaly response procedure.
